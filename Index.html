What is a Fractional CFO?

The story behind your numbers, finally told.

A Fractional CFO gives your business executive-level financial strategy and oversight on a part-time basis, without the expense of hiring a full-time Chief Financial Officer.

At BeyondBalance, we go beyond bookkeeping and reporting. We help business owners understand the story behind their numbers so they can make confident decisions that support growth, profitability, and long-term success.

Financial strategy should be proactive, not reactive. We believe clarity creates confidence.

The Fractional CFO journey

From unclear numbers to a business you steer on purpose.

Stage 01 · Clarity

See where you stand

Financial performance analysis, true margin visibility, and KPI development. We establish exactly where the business is before deciding where it goes.

Stage 02 · Control

Stabilize cash flow

Cash flow forecasting, working capital optimization, and seasonal planning, so cash stops being a monthly surprise and becomes something you manage ahead of time.

Stage 03 · Direction

Plan with confidence

Annual budgets, revenue and profit forecasting, scenario planning, and monthly reviews. Decisions get made on reliable data, not gut feel.

Stage 04 · Growth

Scale on solid ground

Growth strategy, hiring and capital planning, and funding preparation with lender-ready financials. Your systems evolve as the business does.
